1. Introduction

Welcome to Navkar Dispatch NALLP. This Privacy Policy explains how Navkar Apotek LLP ("Company", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when authorized warehouse dispatchers and logistics operators use our Android mobile application Navkar Dispatch NALLP (the "Application" or "App").

Navkar Dispatch NALLP is an enterprise logistics tool designed for pharmaceutical dispatch verification, invoice verification, warehouse parcel loading, and delivery tracking.

2. Information We Access, Collect, and Process

A. Camera and Visual Sensor Data

  • Camera Access (android.permission.CAMERA): The Application requires access to your mobile device's camera exclusively for real-time scanning of:
    • Printed invoice barcodes and QR codes (YYYY-MM-DD + Series + Bill Number).
    • Pharmaceutical product packaging and transport carton barcodes (EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A, UPC-E, Code 128, Code 39, Code 93, Data Matrix, ITF).
  • On-Device Processing: Optical barcode decoding is performed 100% locally on your device using Google ML Kit Barcode Scanning. The camera video feed is processed in-memory and is never recorded, saved as photo files, stored permanently on disk, or transmitted to external servers.
Camera Privacy Notice: The camera feed is only active while the scanner screen is open. It does not record audio or video, and no photos are stored on the device or uploaded to the cloud.

B. Warehouse Staff & Dispatcher Identification

  • Dispatcher Name: The Application allows the warehouse operator to select or input their staff name (e.g., "Vijay Patole").
  • Purpose: This name is recorded in local dispatch logs to maintain accountability for which staff member scanned, verified, and loaded pharmaceutical parcels.

C. Commercial Invoices, Billing, and Medical Store Data

  • Commercial Invoices: When an invoice barcode is scanned or synchronized, the Application queries the company dispatch server to retrieve:
    • Bill Number, Series Code, and Bill Date.
    • Customer / Medical Store Name and Town/Place (e.g., "Pradeep Medicals, Baramati").
    • Total invoice amount and designated transporter/bus service name.
    • Dispatch workflow status (e.g., Started Picking, Packed, Dispatched, Delivered).
  • Customer Directory: The Application synchronizes the customer Account Master directory (/api/AcMaster) containing customer account codes (accode), registered trading names (acname), geographic location (place), and GSTIN (gstno).

D. Device and Network Information

  • Network State (android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, android.permission.INTERNET): Used to monitor network reachability to switch between live server synchronization and offline caching mode.
  • No Device Tracking: We do not collect advertising IDs (AAID), IMEI, MAC addresses, telephony metadata, or personal browser histories.

3. How Information is Used

The information processed by Navkar Dispatch NALLP is used solely for core operational logistics:

  • Dispatch Verification: Ensuring that the correct pharmaceutical parcels and products are loaded for each customer invoice.
  • Double-Scan Prevention: Preventing duplicate scanning or inadvertent double-dispatch of bills.
  • Status Workflow: Updating parcel states from Pending Loading to Loaded and Delivered.
  • Offline Resilience: Storing local copies of customer lists and dispatch logs in the on-device Room database so warehouse operators can continue scanning when Wi-Fi is weak or disconnected.

4. Backend Communication and Data Transfer

  • Server Endpoint: The Application communicates over encrypted HTTPS with the company dispatch server:
    https://dispatch-api.navkarapotek.com
  • Transport Encryption: All communications with the server use Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2 / TLS 1.3) through Cloudflare Edge. Unencrypted cleartext HTTP traffic is completely disabled in the application manifest.
  • Direct Architecture: Network requests occur directly between the mobile application and the designated company dispatch server.

5. Third-Party Services and Analytics

  • No Advertising: Navkar Dispatch NALLP contains zero advertisements. We do not integrate ad networks.
  • No Marketing Trackers: We do not use third-party user behavior trackers, cross-site trackers, or commercial telemetry SDKs.
  • Local Machine Learning: Optical barcode scanning is powered by the Google ML Kit client library, operating on-device.

6. Local Storage, Data Retention, and Deletion

  • Local Database: Dispatch history, employee names, and cached customer directories are stored locally in a private SQLite database managed by Android Room (bill_database).
  • Operator Data Deletion: Users can purge local dispatch records at any time by using the "Clear All Dispatches" feature within the Application.
  • Server Retention: Server-side invoice records and billing histories are maintained in accordance with Indian commercial tax, pharmaceutical regulations, and statutory accounting rules.

7. Children's Privacy

Navkar Dispatch NALLP is strictly an internal enterprise workplace application for wholesale warehouse employees and logistics staff. It is not directed to, marketed to, or designed for children under the age of 13 (or under the age of 18). We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. User Rights and Choices

  • Camera Permission: You may grant or revoke camera permissions at any time via Android System Settings (Settings > Apps > Navkar Dispatch NALLP > Permissions). If revoked, barcode scanning will be disabled, but historical cached records remain viewable.
  • Data Inquiries: For questions regarding customer records or account listings displayed within the directory, warehouse staff may contact the internal system administrator.
